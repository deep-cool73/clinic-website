Featured Treatment

Don't Delay Your Dental Crown After a Root Canal

After root canal treatment, the remaining tooth structure can be weakened. A properly fitted dental crown can help protect the treated tooth and restore function.

Crown placement at our clinic involves precise bite analysis and digital evaluation, carried out by Dr. Priya Gaur (BDS, MIDA), who brings 5+ years of clinical experience to every case.
